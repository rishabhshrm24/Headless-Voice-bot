# Acme Support Knowledge Base

## Business Hours
Acme Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST. We are closed on public holidays.

## Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed within 5-7
business days back to the original payment method. Digital products are non-refundable once
downloaded.

## Subscription Plans
Acme offers three plans: Basic ($9/month), Pro ($29/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing).
Pro plan includes priority support and API access. Enterprise plan includes a dedicated account
manager and SLA guarantees.

## Contacting Support
Customers can reach support via email at support@acme.example or through the in-app chat widget.
Enterprise customers get a dedicated Slack channel for support.

## Password Reset
Users can reset their password from the login page by clicking "Forgot password" and following
the emailed link. The reset link expires after 15 minutes.
