# Acme Support Knowledge Base

## Business Hours
Acme Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST. We are closed on public holidays.

## Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed within 5-7
business days back to the original payment method. Digital products are non-refundable once
downloaded.

## Subscription Plans
Acme offers three plans: Basic ($9/month), Pro ($29/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing).
Pro plan includes priority support and API access. Enterprise plan includes a dedicated account
manager and SLA guarantees.

## Contacting Support
Customers can reach support via email at support@acme.example or through the in-app chat widget.
Enterprise customers get a dedicated Slack channel for support.

## Password Reset
Users can reset their password from the login page by clicking "Forgot password" and following
the emailed link. The reset link expires after 15 minutes.

## Billing and Invoices
Invoices are generated on the 1st of every month for active subscriptions. Customers can download
past invoices from the billing section in their account dashboard. Failed payments are retried
automatically up to three times before the account is paused.

## Account Cancellation
Customers can cancel their subscription at any time from the account settings page. Cancellation
takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and any unused time is not refunded.

## Technical Troubleshooting
If the app is not loading, customers should first check their internet connection and browser
version, then refresh the page. If issues persist, they can contact support with a screenshot and
description of the error. Common issues include login loops, failed API requests, and delayed
notifications.

## Data Privacy
Acme stores customer data securely and does not share personal information with third parties
without consent, except where required by law. Customers can request a copy of their stored data
or deletion of their account from the privacy settings page.

## Common Error Codes
- E101: Invalid login credentials. Ask the customer to confirm their email and password, then use
  "Forgot password" if needed.
- E202: Payment failed. The billing system could not process the transaction. Ask the customer to
  check card details or retry later.
- E303: Subscription expired. The account is no longer active. Offer renewal or plan upgrade options.
- E404: Resource not found. This usually means the requested page, document, or account record does
  not exist or the user does not have access.
- E429: Too many requests. The user has hit a rate limit. Ask them to wait a few minutes and retry.
- E500: Internal server error. This is a platform issue. Tell the customer that the team has been
  notified and advise them to retry after some time.
- E503: Service unavailable. The system is temporarily down for maintenance or overload.
